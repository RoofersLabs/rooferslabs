## 1. Executive Summary

### Purpose

RoofersLabs exists to eliminate one of the largest sources of lost revenue for roofing companies: unanswered inbound customer calls.

Every inbound call represents a potential business opportunity. When calls go unanswered, customers often contact another contractor, resulting in lost revenue, reduced marketing effectiveness, and a poor customer experience.

RoofersLabs provides an AI-powered Front Office Platform that answers every inbound call, understands customer intent, captures structured information, qualifies opportunities, detects emergencies, answers company-specific questions, and notifies roofing companies with complete conversation records.

---

### Core Problem

Roofing companies spend most of their working hours:

- Visiting job sites
- Inspecting properties
- Meeting customers
- Supervising crews
- Managing projects

As a result, customer calls are frequently missed.

---

### Solution

RoofersLabs acts as an AI-powered front office that:

- Answers every inbound call
- Conducts natural conversations
- Captures customer information
- Qualifies leads
- Detects emergencies
- Collects appointment requests
- Generates AI summaries
- Notifies the business immediately

---

### MVP Principle

> No roofing company should lose a qualified customer because nobody answered the phone.

Every product and engineering decision should reinforce this principle.

---

### Long-Term Vision

Build the AI operating layer for the roofing industry by expanding from inbound call management into a complete customer communication and business operations platform.
